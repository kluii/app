**What this commit does and does not do.** The transport and the registry are
complete: all 21 frozen names, their schemas, their error shapes. `whoami`,
`list_boards` and `get_board` answer for real. The remaining 18 handlers land in
K4-03 (boards), K4-04 (columns and cards) and K4-05 (reading, comments, search,
activity, export), in that order; until then each answers `-32603` naming the
milestone that fills it rather than pretending to have worked. That staging is
the roadmap's own split, not a deviation from the exit criteria above.
